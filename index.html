Payout structure (ROI credits)

Accrual period

Returns are calculated based on the performance of your selected portfolio during each cycle.

Credit frequency

Profits are credited to your available balance every 14 days (bi-weekly).

Rollover (compounding)

You can choose to automatically roll over your bi-weekly credits into principal to accelerate compounding.

Payout (withdrawal)

Alternatively, set credits to payout so your bi-weekly ROI becomes available for withdrawal as it’s credited.

Risk disclosure

  • No guarantees: ROI credits depend on portfolio performance.
  • Capital risk: you may lose some or all of principal.
  • Volatility & liquidity: timing/availability may vary based on terms and market conditions.

Only invest what you can afford to lose. This site is informational, not financial advice.

FAQ

Is ROI guaranteed?

No. ROI credits are performance-based and not guaranteed. Losses are possible.

How often are profits credited?

Every 14 days (bi-weekly), based on portfolio performance during the cycle.

Can I compound instead of withdrawing?

Yes. You can set credits to “rollover” to compound, or “payout” to withdraw eligible credited ROI.

What happens to my principal?

Your principal remains active within the portfolio framework. Details and any liquidity rules are confirmed during onboarding.
