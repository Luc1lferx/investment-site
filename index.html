Overview

What this is

A private investment structure offering curated portfolio participation with defined payout cadence. You choose a plan tier and receive periodic profit credits based on the performance of your selected portfolio.

Core principle

Clarity over hype. Terms are stated upfront, credits follow a consistent schedule, and options are simple: rollover to compound or payout to withdraw eligible credited ROI.

Who it’s for

For investors comfortable with market risk who want structure, visibility, and a clean, premium process from start to finish.

“At Vantage Bridge, we believe in consistent momentum. Once you activate your account with a minimum of $500, our automated strategies begin working immediately. Payout structure (ROI credits)

Accrual period

Returns are calculated based on the performance of your selected portfolio during each cycle.

Credit frequency

Profits are credited to your available balance every 14 days (bi-weekly).

Rollover (compounding option)

Choose to automatically roll over your bi-weekly ROI credit into principal to accelerate compounding.

Payout (withdrawal option)

Choose payout so your bi-weekly ROI credit becomes available for withdrawal when credited (subject to terms).

Risks (read this)

  • No guarantees: ROI credits depend on portfolio performance.
  • Capital risk: you can lose some or all of your investment.
  • Volatility: markets can move unpredictably.
  • Liquidity: timing/availability can be subject to the agreed terms and operational processing.

FAQ

Is ROI guaranteed?

No. ROI credits are performance-based and not guaranteed. Losses are possible.

How often are profits credited?

Every 14 days (bi-weekly), based on portfolio performance during the cycle.

Can I compound instead of withdrawing?

Yes. Set credits to “rollover” to compound, or “payout” to withdraw eligible credited ROI (subject to terms).

What happens to my principal?

Your principal remains active within the selected portfolio framework. Any liquidity rules are confirmed during onboarding.
